⚔️ This Week — Matchups

New York Titans def. Big Dick Drew 122.68 – 106.44
Drew actually won the Sunday afternoon slate outright — nearly 92 points banked in the early window alone, more than the Titans put up all day Sunday. But the Titans had already stashed 26.7 points from a Wednesday game before the weekend even started, then got a Sunday night boost from Malik Nabers to close it out. Drew had nothing left once the late windows wrapped — no Sunday night, no Monday, and the early lead wasn't enough to survive it.
Better Call Hall def. Titsburgh Feelers 160.84 – 102.7
Over before it started. Bijan Robinson, Zay Flowers, Breece Hall and Tyler Shough combined for a 127.5-point Sunday afternoon, and the game was functionally decided by the early window alone. Titsburgh actually put together a real Sunday night push — Jaxson Dart and Dak Prescott combined for 44.6 points after dark — but a 44-point hole from that morning was never getting closed.
Love is in the Air def. Don Dynasty 147.2 – 96.6
The deficit was built before Sunday even arrived — Love's Wednesday/Thursday pieces (Stafford, Adams, Stevenson) had already banked 30.8 points while Don's corresponding early-week piece, Romeo Doubs, scored a zero. Derrick Henry's 35.3 on Sunday afternoon buried it further. Don got a strong Sunday night game from Isaiah Likely (27.8) to make the final number respectable, but this one was decided well before that.
East Coast Express def. New York Nets 163.32 – 146.0
The Nets were actually up 131.7 to 106.6 heading into Monday Night Football — a real, comfortable lead built across the whole Sunday slate. Then Kenneth Walker and Patrick Mahomes, both playing that Monday night, combined for 56.8 points for East Coast Express and flipped the entire game in the last three hours of the week. The Nets' lone Monday piece, Evan Engram, wasn't close to enough to answer.
Shanghai Assassins def. Popcorn Carnells 161.86 – 62.32
The week's most lopsided result by a wide margin. Shanghai was already up 30.7-12.8 by Thursday night, then Jonathan Taylor, Chris Olave, Mike Evans and Bucky Irving turned Sunday afternoon into a formality. Popcorn Carnells never scored more than 27 points in any single window all week.
Lead Them To Paradise def. Kim Jong Un 145.12 – 144.96
Game of the week, decided by 0.16 points. Kim Jong Un's week was already finished by Sunday night at 144.96 — Christian Watson and Jayden Daniels both went off in the late window to build what looked like a winning number. Paradise still trailed 134.02 to 144.96 heading into Monday Night Football, needing a near-exact number from Rashee Rice and Jaylen Waddle to survive. They got exactly enough — 11.1 combined points — to escape by the smallest possible margin.
